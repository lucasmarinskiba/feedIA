## GENERATOR LOGIC

**Step 1**: Analyze inputs (positioning + customer + unique advantage)

**Step 2**: Generate 10 distinct offer angles using different frameworks:

1. **Authority** angle (credibility-first)
2. **Problem** angle (pain-first)
3. **Result** angle (benefit-first)
4. **Social Proof** angle (tribe-first)
5. **Transformation** angle (before-after)
6. **Comparison** angle (vs competitor)
7. **Scarcity** angle (limited-time)
8. **Risk Reversal** angle (guarantee-first)
9. **Community** angle (belonging)
10. **Premium** angle (luxury/exclusivity)

**Step 3**: For each offer, generate:

- Positioning statement (1 sentence)
- Hero narrative (customer problem → solution → outcome)
- 3-tier pricing (anchor high, reveal lower)
- 5 objection scripts (common pushback + response)
- Key messaging (SUCCESs framework applied)
