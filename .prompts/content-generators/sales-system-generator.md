# Sales System Generator

**Purpose**: Input prospect profile + offer → Output complete repeatable sales system (100 customers in 90 days).

**Timeline**: 2 hours (from inputs → ready-to-execute sales playbook).

**Uses**: Sales System Full SKILL + Holmes 12 strategies + Voss FBI negotiation + Cialdini 6 weapons + SUCCESs messaging.

---

## INPUT SECTION

**1. Target Prospect Profile**

- Role/title: _________________
- Company size: _________________
- Pain points (3): _________________
- Budget authority: _________________
- Decision timeline: _________________

**2. Your Offer**

- Product/service (1 sentence): _________________
- Unique advantage: _________________
- Price range (3-tier): $_____ / $_____ / $_____

**3. Top 20 Prospects** (List names, emails, urgency level)

---

## GENERATOR LOGIC

**Step 1**: Define 7-touch sequence

- Touch 1: Personalized opening email
- Touch 2: Phone call (15 min qualification)
- Touch 3: Demo/meeting (30 min)
- Touch 4: Post-demo follow-up
- Touch 5: Trial usage check-in
- Touch 6: Trial-to-customer negotiation
- Touch 7: Onboarding + retention

**Step 2**: Generate scripts for each touch

- Email templates (personalization hooks)
- Call scripts (discovery questions + positioning)
- Demo walkthrough (pain → solution → outcome)
- Objection responses (3 per objection)

**Step 3**: Build objection playbook

- 10 common objections
- 3 responses each (probe, reframe, commit)

**Step 4**: Metrics dashboard

- Weekly tracking (reaches, calls, demos, trials, closes)
- Conversion targets (20% email response, 50% call-to-demo, 60% demo-to-trial, 70% trial-to-close)

## OUTPUT TEMPLATE

### COMPLETE SALES SYSTEM (90 Days, 100 Customers)

**Prospect Profile**: Head of Ops, 50-500 person SaaS, $20K/month tool budget, high urgency

**Offer**: "Cut 10 hours/week task admin. $50/mo. Try free 30 days."

---

**PHASE 1: PROSPECTING** (Weeks 1-2)

**Top 20 Prospect List**:

| Name   | Company     | Email          | Urgency | Contact Method |
| ------ | ----------- | -------------- | ------- | -------------- |
| Sarah  | Acme Corp   | sarah@acme.com | High    | Email → Call   |
| Marcus | Startup XYZ | marcus@xyz.com | Medium  | Email → Call   |
| ...    | ...         | ...            | ...     | ...            |

**Outreach Goal**: 20 prospects → 6-8 calls scheduled (30% response rate)

---

**TOUCH 1: OPENING EMAIL** (Day 1)

**Template** (personalize for each prospect):

> Sarah — Saw Acme hit 500 people (congrats!). Our founder managed ops at 300-person company, faced same Asana chaos you mentioned [in article/tweet].
>
> We built simple task management for ops teams. Saves 10 hours/week (based on similar companies).
>
> Free 15-min audit? Available Tuesday 10am?

**Metrics**: Aim for 20-30% response rate (6-8 from 20).

---

**TOUCH 2: PHONE CALL** (Day 3, if response)

**Script** (15 minutes):

- 1 min: Rapport ("Hey Sarah, how's your week?")
- 2 min: Permission ("Got 15 min? Appreciate it.")
- 3 min: Discovery (ask hard questions):
  - "What's hardest about [current tool]?"
  - "How are you solving this now?"
  - "When was last time you considered changing?"
  - "If you fix ONE thing, what?"
- 5 min: Positioning ("Here's how others solved it...")
- 3 min: Next step ("Make sense? 30-min demo together?")
- 1 min: Close (send calendar link)

**Green lights** (invest more):

- Specific pain ("20 hours/week admin")
- Urgency ("Need to fix this month")
- Decision power ("I control budget")

**Red flags** (stop investing):

- "Happy with current tool" + zero urgency
- Can't articulate problem
- "Need to ask my boss" (gatekeeper, not buyer)

**Conversion goal**: 5-6 calls → 4-5 demos (60% call-to-demo)

---

**TOUCH 3: DEMO/MEETING** (Day 7)

**Script** (30 minutes):

- 5 min: Their use case ("Walk me through typical week")
- 10 min: Live demo (show THEIR workflow, not features)
  - Lead with pain: "You mentioned 20 hrs/week admin. Watch how we cut that to 10..."
  - Show outcomes not features: "This feature saves 10 hrs/week because [outcome they care about]"
- 10 min: Q&A ("Does that make sense?")
- 5 min: Close ("Want to try 30-day trial, no CC?")

**Objections that come up** + responses:

- "Looks good but need to evaluate more"
  → "Perfect, try free trial. Let's reconnect Friday?"
- "I need to talk to my team"
  → "Make sense. Who else should I loop in?"
- "Too expensive"
  → "Let's get you in trial first, then we'll discuss pricing if it works."

**Close language**: "This saves your team 10 hours/week. How about we lock in 30-day trial, no credit card? You'll see results. Then let's talk pricing."

**Conversion goal**: 4-5 demos → 3-4 trial signups (70% demo-to-trial)

---

**TOUCH 4: POST-DEMO FOLLOW-UP** (Day 10)

**Email** (summarize + create action):

> Sarah — Great discussion Friday. Few things we covered:
>
> 1. You need to cut task admin time
> 2. Simplicity > features (you said it!)
> 3. Your team's buy-in is key
>
> Here's what's next:
>
> - I've created your trial (link)
> - Setup takes 30 min (I'll hop on if you want)
> - Try focus mode (that's your pain point)
> - Let's reconnect Wed to see how it's going
>
> Sarah, confident this solves your 20-hour problem. Let's validate it together.

**If no response after 2 days**: Call again. "Hi Sarah, not sure if email landed. Following up on trial access — want to jump on quick call?"

**Conversion goal**: Keep momentum → trial activation

---

**TOUCH 5: TRIAL USAGE CHECK-IN** (Day 14)

**Call/message**:

> Sarah — Quick check-in. Did you get a chance to try?
>
> - How's setup going?
> - Used focus mode yet? (This is your main pain point)
> - Any blockers?
>
> I'm here to help. Let me know if you want 15-min walkthrough.

**If used it** (good sign):

- "What do you think? Saving time?" (let them sell)
- "What could be better?" (objections)
- "Ready to commit to extended trial?" (micro-commitment)

**If didn't use it** (bad sign):

- "What got in the way?"
- "Can we do 15-min walkthrough together?" (remove friction)
- If still no interest: "Not right fit now? Keep in touch in 3 months?"

**Conversion goal**: 3-4 trials → 2-3 with positive usage (80% usage rate)

---

**TOUCH 6: TRIAL-TO-CUSTOMER NEGOTIATION** (Day 18, if positive usage)

**Email/call**:

> Sarah — You've been using it 5 days. Results:
>
> - 10 hours/week saved (vs your goal)
> - Team using it daily (good adoption signal)
>
> Let's close. Can you commit to 30-day paid plan?
>
> Options:
>
> - $50/mo (your use case, best value)
> - $99/mo (if team expands)
> - Custom if specific needs
>
> Which works?

**Negotiation tactics** (Voss):

| Objection         | Tactic         | Response                                           |
| ----------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| "Too expensive"   | Mirror + probe | "Too expensive?" → "What would work?"              |
| "Need to approve" | Empathy        | "CFO approval is blocker?" → "What does CFO need?" |
| "Need more time"  | Scarcity       | "Early pricing this month only. Then goes to $99"  |

**Close language**: "You've seen it works. Team's using it. Saving 10 hours/week. Let's do $50/mo, 30 days. Cancel anytime. Deal?"

**Conversion goal**: 2-3 active trials → 2-3 closed deals (100% conversion)

---

**TOUCH 7: ONBOARDING + RETENTION** (Day 21, deal closed)

**Email**:

> Sarah — Welcome! Thrilled to have Acme Corp on board.
>
> Next:
>
> 1. Onboarding call tomorrow 10am (30 min, walk team through)
> 2. I'm your POC (point of contact)
> 3. 30-day check-in (make sure it works)
>
> First goal: 10 hrs/week savings by end of week 2.

**Follow-up (Day 30)**:

> Sarah — 30-day check-in:
>
> Metrics from your account:
>
> - Team active 5/5 days ✓
> - Daily focus mode used ✓
> - 11 hours/week saved (beat goal!) ✓
>
> Let's extend to 3-month plan? Same $50/mo.

**Retention goal**: Close → onboard → expand (NRR >100%)
