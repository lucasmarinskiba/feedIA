# Community Charter Generator

**Purpose**: Input community purpose + target member → Output complete 12-month community blueprint (vision + identity + SPACES audit + leadership pipeline).

**Timeline**: 2 hours (from input → ready-to-launch community charter).

**Uses**: Community Flywheel SKILL + Richardson 3 stages + Spinks SPACES + Carnegie appreciation.

---

## INPUT SECTION

**1. Community Purpose** (Why it exists)

- Example: "Help solo founders ship products faster via peer accountability + feedback"

**Your answer**: _________________________________________________

---

**2. Target Member** (Specific persona)

- Role: _________________
- Pain points: _________________
- Aspirations: _________________

---

**3. Community Identity**

- Name: _________________
- Core values (3-4): _________________
- Weekly ritual: _________________

---

**4. 12-Month Growth Goal**

- Month 1: _____ members
- Month 6: _____ members
- Month 12: _____ members

---

## GENERATOR LOGIC

**Step 1**: Define 3 phases (Spark/Stoke/Pass)

- Spark (months 1-3): Build identity + founding cohort
- Stoke (months 4-6): Distribute leadership + audit outcomes
- Pass (months 7-12): Exponential growth + self-sustaining

**Step 2**: Generate charter

- Vision (WHO/WHY/IDENTITY)
- Recruitment strategy (how to find members)
- Weekly ritual (what members do together)
- Leadership pipeline (who to recruit as leaders)
- SPACES audit (6 outcomes to optimize)

**Step 3**: Design 12-month roadmap

- Months 1-3: 50 → 100 members
- Months 4-6: 100 → 300 members
- Months 7-12: 300 → 1000 members

**Step 4**: Generate metrics + pivot triggers

- Retention (70%+ weekly active)
- NPS (50+)
- Referral rate (20%+ from referrals)
- Leader count (3-5 by month 6, 7-10 by month 12)

## OUTPUT TEMPLATE

### 12-MONTH COMMUNITY CHARTER

**Community Name**: Indie SaaS Builders

**Vision**: "Community for solo founders to ship products together, get feedback, and hit $1K MRR faster."

**Target Members**:

- Solo founders, indie hackers, bootstrappers
- Building SaaS solo
- Want accountability + co-founder search + peer support

**Identity**:

- Logo: Ship emoji 🚢
- Values: Shipping, learning, helping, transparency
- Weekly ritual: "Ship Day" Zoom call (30 min, share progress + feedback)
- Monthly ritual: Member spotlight (case study video)

---

**PHASE 1: SPARK** (Months 1-3, 50 → 100 members)

**Recruitment**:

- Personal outreach: 20 DMs to known founders
- ProductHunt + Indie Hackers: 30-50 signups
- Twitter threads: 10-30 early adopters
- Goal: 50 founding members (Week 1-4)

**Weekly Ritual (Ship Day)**:

- 2 min: Welcome
- 2 min each: 5-7 members share 2-min update
- 5 min: Q&A
- 5 min: Random breakout (1-on-1)
- 2 min: Close

**Identity Lock** (Weeks 5-8):

- Vote on name
- Define values
- Lock community charter
- 1 sub-leader emerging

**Success Metrics** (Month 3):

- ☐ 50 founding members
- ☐ 70%+ attendance
- ☐ Charter locked
- ☐ 1 leader emerging

---

**PHASE 2: STOKE** (Months 4-6, 100 → 300 members)

**Leader Identification** (Month 4):

- Ravi: Helper (answers Q&A) → "Lead Friday threads?"
- Priya: Organizer (runs events) → "Organize monthly meetup?"
- Anmol: Speaker (shares stories) → "Record case studies?"

**Leader Supercharge** (Month 5):

- Give autonomy: "You own this, full freedom"
- Visibility: Public credit
- Tools: Templates, moderation powers

**SPACES Audit** (Month 5):

| Outcome      | Rating | Investment         |
| ------------ | ------ | ------------------ |
| Support      | 8/10   | Monitor            |

| Acquisition  | 5/10   | Referral program   |

**Referral Program** (Month 5):

- "Refer a founder → 1 month free"
- Goal: 40% of new members from referrals by month 6

**Success Metrics** (Month 6):

- ☐ 300 members (100 → 300)
- ☐ 3-5 distributed leaders
- ☐ SPACES audit complete
- ☐ Referral program launched
- ☐ Retention 75%+

---

**PHASE 3: PASS + EXPONENTIAL** (Months 7-12, 300 → 1000 members)

**Distributed Leadership** (Month 7):

- Founder facilitates (not creates)
- Leaders run rituals

**Flywheel Momentum** (Month 7-9):

- Turn 4 (month 9): Self-sustaining
- Referral rate: 20%+ (passive growth)

**Growth Channels** (Month 7-9):

**Success Metrics** (Month 12):

- ☐ 1000 members (300 → 1000, exponential)

- ☐ Self-sustaining (founder 2 hrs/week)
