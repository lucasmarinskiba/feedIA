# Growth Plan Generator

**Purpose**: Input growth target + positioning → Output complete 90-day growth strategy (Bullseye channels, DEAL automation, testing sprints, metrics dashboard).

**Timeline**: 1 hour (from input → ready-to-execute 90-day plan).

**Uses**: Growth Engine SKILL + Ferriss DEAL + Weinberg Bullseye + Ariely psychology.

---

## INPUT SECTION

**1. Growth Target** (Day 90)

- Followers/subscribers: ______
- Leads: ______
- Customers: ______
- Revenue (MRR): ______

Example: 10K followers, 1000 leads, 100 customers, $5K MRR

---

**2. Current State** (Day 0)

- Followers: ______
- Email list: ______
- Customers: ______
- Revenue: ______

---

**3. Product/Service**

- What are you selling? (1 sentence): _________________

---

**4. Target Customer**

- Who's your ideal buyer? _________________

---

**5. Unique Positioning**

- What's different? _________________

---

## GENERATOR LOGIC

**Step 1**: Calculate growth needed (target - current = gap)

Example: 0 → 10K followers = 10K follower goal

**Step 2**: Apply Ferriss DEAL

- Definition: Growth target + timeline (above)
- Elimination: Identify tactics NOT to do (80% of effort = 20% of results)
- Automation: What systems run without founder?
- Liberation: How do customers bring customers?

**Step 3**: Apply Weinberg Bullseye

- Brainstorm 19 channels
- Rank by effort/reach/confidence
- Pick top 3 inner-ring channels
- Design 2-week sprint tests for each

**Step 4**: Design 12-week roadmap

- Weeks 1-2: Test channel A
- Weeks 3-4: Test channel B
- Weeks 5-6: Test channel C
- Weeks 7-12: Scale winning channel 3-5x

**Step 5**: Generate metrics dashboard

- Weekly tracking (followers, leads, conversions)
- Pivot triggers (when to change channels)
- Success thresholds (% of goal = acceptable)
