## Section 2: SPARK PHASE (Months 1-3): Build Identity + Founding Cohort

**Goal**: Recruit 50 founding members. Lock identity. Weekly ritual becomes habit.

### Recruitment (Weeks 1-4)

**Target**: 50 founding members (most passionate, connected, willing to participate weekly)

**Recruitment channels**:

- Personal outreach (10-20 DMs to known builders)
- ProductHunt + Indie Hackers posts (30-50 signups)
- Twitter threads (10-30 retweets / early adopters)
- Email to past customers (5-10 referrals)

**Recruitment message**:

> "Building community for indie SaaS founders. Weekly 'Ship Day' call: 30 min, show progress, get feedback, meet peers. First 50 founders only (intentional). Free. Interested? [link]"

### Weekly Ritual (Ship Day — Weeks 1-12)

**Structure** (30 minutes):

1. **2 min**: Welcome + who's here (5-15 founders)
2. **2 min each**: Member share (5-7 people share 2-min update: what shipped, what stuck)
3. **5 min**: Q&A (anyone ask question to presenters)
4. **5 min**: Random breakout (pair up, chat 1-on-1)
5. **2 min**: Close + next week's date

**Tone**: Peer-to-peer (NO lectures, NO selling, NO pitching). Founder runs it (not hired host).

**Attendance goal**: 70%+ weekly (shows stickiness).

### Identity Lock (Weeks 5-8)

**Input from community**:

- "What should we call ourselves?" (vote or consensus)
- "What are our values?" (open discussion)
- "What should this community help us achieve?" (map desired outcomes)

**Output**: Community charter (1-page document with name, values, mission, rituals, code of conduct).

### Metrics (Month 3 Target)

- ☐ 50 founding members recruited
- ☐ 70%+ attendance on Ship Day (10-15 per week)
- ☐ Community charter locked (name, values, rituals defined)
- ☐ 1 sub-leader emerging (organizer, helper, speaker)
- ☐ First community win (2 members found co-founder, or 1 hit $1K MRR)

## Section 3: STOKE PHASE (Months 4-6): Distribute Leadership + SPACES Audit

**Goal**: Grow to 300 members. Identify + supercharge 3-5 leaders. Audit SPACES outcomes.

### Leader Identification (Month 4)

**Question**: Who keeps showing up? Who helps others?

**Types of leaders**:

1. **Helpers** (answer questions in chat, give feedback generously)
2. **Organizers** (run events, create rituals, coordinate)
3. **Speakers** (share case studies, teach skills, build authority)

**Your leaders identified**:

| Name  | Type      | Current role                 | Supercharge ask                    |
| ----- | --------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Ravi  | Helper    | Passive lurker →             | "Lead Q&A thread Fridays?"         |
| Priya | Organizer | Suggested in-person meetup → | "Organize monthly NYC meetup?"     |
| Anmol | Speaker   | Shared journey →             | "Record monthly case study video?" |
|       |           |                              |                                    |

### Leader Supercharging (Month 4-5)

**Give them**: Autonomy + Visibility + Tools.

**Autonomy**: "Ravi, you own Friday Q&A thread. Full freedom. I trust you."

**Visibility**: Public credit. "Thanks to our leaders: Ravi (Q&A), Priya (meetups), Anmol (case studies)."

**Tools**: Templates, moderation powers (if online), budget (if offline event).

**Outcome**: Leaders feel ownership → community runs without founder.

### SPACES Audit (Month 5)

**Rate each SPACES outcome** (1-10 scale):

| SPACES Outcome   | Definition                                                 | Current Rating | Underserved? | Action Plan      |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ------------ | ---------------- |
| **Support**      | Members helping members? Q&A active? Mentoring?            | _/10           | ☐ Yes ☐ No   | If yes: [action] |
| **Product**      | Community influencing product roadmap? Feedback loops?     | _/10           | ☐ Yes ☐ No   | If yes: [action] |
| **Acquisition**  | WOM referrals working? New members from community?         | _/10           | ☐ Yes ☐ No   | If yes: [action] |
| **Contribution** | UGC strong (case studies, guides, templates)? Co-creation? | _/10           | ☐ Yes ☐ No   | If yes: [action] |
| **Engagement**   | 70%+ monthly active? Sticky? Return rate high?             | _/10           | ☐ Yes ☐ No   | If yes: [action] |
| **Success**      | Members achieving their goals (outcomes tracked)?          | _/10           | ☐ Yes ☐ No   | If yes: [action] |

**Example**:

- Support: 8/10 (members answer each other's questions)
- Product: 4/10 (no product feedback loop → ACTION: monthly "feature request" thread)
- Acquisition: 5/10 (some referrals, not systematic → ACTION: formal referral program)
- Contribution: 6/10 (few case studies → ACTION: monthly spotlights + incentives for UGC)
- Engagement: 8/10 (70% weekly active in Ship Day)
- Success: 9/10 (members hitting $1K MRR, finding co-founders)

### Investment Plan (Months 5-6)

**Pick 2-3 underserved SPACES outcomes** → invest energy there.

Example:

- Acquisition: Launch referral program ("Refer a friend → 1 month free")
- Contribution: Start member spotlight series (video case studies)
- Product: If building SaaS, create product feedback loop

### Metrics (Month 6 Target)

- ☐ 300 members (50 → 300)
- ☐ 3-5 distributed leaders (each with clear role)
- ☐ SPACES audit complete (2-3 underserved outcomes identified)
- ☐ Referral program launched (members bring members)
- ☐ UGC content growing (member case studies, guides)
- ☐ Retention 75%+ (members staying engaged)

## Section 4: PASS PHASE (Months 7-9): Flywheel Momentum + Self-Sustaining

**Goal**: Grow to 500 members. Leadership distributed. Founder facilitates, members drive.

### Founder Role Shift (Month 7)

**Before**: Founder creates content, hosts calls, organizes everything.

**After**: Members create content. Leaders host calls. Founder facilitates + removes blockers.

**Specific actions**:

- Ravi leads Q&A (not founder)
- Priya organizes meetups (founder just attends)
- Anmol records case studies (founder edits, shares)
- Community handles referrals (founder just celebrates)

**Founder time required**: 5 hours/week → 2 hours/week (strategic only).

### Flywheel Momentum (Collins Framework)

**Consistent push** (every week):

- Ship Day call (Ravi leads, but founder attends)
- Q&A thread (Ravi moderates)
- Shout-outs (founder posts weekly wins)
- Spotlight (monthly case study)

**With each push** (compound effect):

- Members feel seen → invite friends
- Content shared externally → new members find you
- Leaders feel trusted → recruit more leaders
- Outcomes achieved → word-of-mouth

**Turn 4** (month 9) = self-sustaining (push 4 generates momentum without needing founder push).

### Growth Channel Amplification (Months 7-9)

**Leverage community as traction channel**:

| Channel          | How Community Amplifies                                            |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Email**        | Member case studies → email content (high credibility)             |
| **Social media** | Community stories → viral assets (authentic UGC)                   |
| **Referrals**    | Referral program + Word-of-mouth → 20% of new members              |
| **Content**      | Members create guides/templates → library grows                    |
| **Partnerships** | Community knows complementary founders → partnership opportunities |

### Metrics (Month 9 Target)

- ☐ 500 members (300 → 500, 67% growth)
- ☐ 5-7 sub-leaders (distributed leadership deep)
- ☐ 50% of new members from referrals (passive growth)
- ☐ UGC = 70% of content (members creating more than founder)
- ☐ Founder works 2 hrs/week (system runs without them)
- ☐ Community drives 20-30% of product customers (if SaaS)
- ☐ Retention 80%+ (stickiness high)

## Section 5: SCALE PHASE (Months 10-12): Exponential + Sustainability

**Goal**: Grow to 1000 members. Breakeven (self-sustaining). 2-year vision locked.

### Exponential Curve (Each turn = 2x)

**Flywheel physics**:

- Turn 1 (month 3): 50 members
- Turn 2 (month 6): 100 members
- Turn 3 (month 9): 300 members (compound accelerates)
- Turn 4 (month 12): 1000 members (exponential inflection)

**Why**: Once you have product-market fit (community loves it), word-of-mouth + referrals compound.

### Monetization (Optional, Month 10+)

**If community is core to business model**:

- Sponsorship (tools/services sponsor monthly to reach 1000 members)
- Paid tier (free for core members, $50-100/year for premium benefits)
- Affiliate referrals (recommend tools, earn commission)
- Course/mastermind (premium education for members)

**Community first** (never commercialize before strong engagement).

### 2-Year Vision (Month 12)

**Questions to answer**:

- Will community stay online only, or add in-person meetups?
- Will you expand to multiple communities (vertical expansion)?
- Will community become primary revenue driver?
- Who will be permanent community steward (might not be you)?
