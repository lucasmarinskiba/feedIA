## Section 3: INFLUENCE STACK (6 Cialdini Weapons)

**Input**: Build 3-4 influence weapons into every pitch touchpoint.

### Stacking Rules

- Max 3-4 weapons per pitch (too many = feels salesy)
- Each weapon should feel natural (not forced)
- Stack different weapons across touchpoints (day 1: authority, day 3: liking, day 7: reciprocity)

### 6 Weapons Filled

| Weapon           | Definition                            | Your Implementation             | Example                                                   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Authority**    | Credentials, proof, specifics         | Who/what validates you?         | "ADHD founder. 8/10 NPS from 500+ beta users."            |
| **Liking**       | Similarity, compliments, shared goals | What makes customer like you?   | "Designed BY people with ADHD, FOR people with ADHD."     |
| **Reciprocity**  | Give value first (creates obligation) | What free value you give first? | "Free ADHD 30-day productivity challenge."                |
| **Commitment**   | Small ask → escalates to big ask      | What's the smallest yes?        | "Try free 14-day trial?" → "Extend to 30?" → "Subscribe?" |
| **Social proof** | "People like us do things like us"    | Who's using it already?         | "500+ indie makers, VPs of Ops, solopreneurs."            |
| **Scarcity**     | Real/perceived limited availability   | What's limited (real)?          | "Early founder pricing ($30/mo) → $50 next month."        |

### Influence Stack by Touchpoint

**Touchpoint 1** (Day 1 cold email): Authority + Reciprocity

> "Built by ADHD founder. 8/10 NPS from 500+ makers. Here's free ADHD challenge: [link]"

**Touchpoint 2** (Day 3 phone call): Liking + Social proof

> "One of our founders had exact same problem you mentioned. 200+ ops teams now use this."

**Touchpoint 3** (Day 7 follow-up): Scarcity + Commitment

> "Early founder pricing ends next month. Can you commit to 30-day trial this week?"

**Touchpoint 4** (Negotiation): Reciprocity + Commitment

> "We've given custom onboarding (value). Can you commit to 3-month plan?"

## Section 4: PRICING PSYCHOLOGY (Poundstone + Ariely)

**Input**: Design 3-tier pricing + anchoring strategy.

### Pricing Anchor Strategy

**Anchor high first** (shows value → everything else cheap)

| Tier           | Price   | Features                                     | Target             | Anchor Effect                      |
| -------------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| **Enterprise** | $500/mo | Full API, dedicated support, custom features | Large teams (500+) | Anchor (highest price shown first) |
| **Pro**        | $99/mo  | Full features, priority support              | Mid teams (50-200) | Feels reasonable (vs $500)         |
| **Starter**    | $50/mo  | Core features, standard support              | Small teams (1-20) | Feels like bargain (vs $99)        |
| **Early bird** | $30/mo  | Same as Starter, limited time                | New customers      | Creates scarcity + urgency         |

**Behavioral triggers**:

- Show Enterprise first (anchor perception high)
- Reveal lower tiers → feels like bargain
- Early bird = scarcity (increases urgency)
- Use "per month" not "annual" = lower sticker shock

### Pricing Justification

**Question**: Why these prices (not cheaper/more expensive)?

**Economic answer**: [Your cost structure + margin target]
Example: "30% cost of goods, 30% operations, 40% profit/growth = need $50 minimum."

**Psychological answer**: [What signal does price send?]
Example: "Premium price ($50+) signals quality. Cheap ($10) signals 'meh.' $99 signals 'professional tool.'"

**Market answer**: [What do competitors charge? What can market bear?]
Example: "Asana = $10-25/user. We're $30-500/team. Premium positioning justified by ADHD-specific design."

## Section 5: BEHAVIORAL TRIGGERS (Ariely Expectations)

**Input**: How do expectations shape perception? What signals matter?

### Expectation Setting

**Question**: What word/image/proof makes customer perceive high value?

| Trigger                 | Your Signal                            | Example                                         | Effect                         |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Word signal**         | What word increases perceived value?   | "AI-powered" (not "automated")                  | 3x perceived value             |
| **Visual signal**       | What design/image increases trust?     | Minimalist (not cluttered)                      | Clean = premium                |
| **Social proof signal** | What numbers increase confidence?      | "500+ users" (not "thousands")                  | Specific numbers = credible    |
| **Speed signal**        | How fast to success?                   | "30-min setup" (not "easy")                     | Concrete timeline = believable |
| **Result signal**       | What concrete outcome proves it works? | "Save 10 hours/week" (not "be more productive") | Measurable = credible          |

**NOT**: "Save 10 hours/week"
**YES**: "Don't lose 10 hours/week to task overwhelm"

Why? Loss hurts ~2x more than gain satisfies (Ariely).

## Section 6: OFFER TESTING (30 Days)

**Input**: How will you test offer converts?

### Test 1: Sticky Hook Testing (Week 1)

**Message**: [Your 1-sentence sticky hook]

> "Stop task overwhelm. Ship 2x faster with ADHD-friendly task management."

**Test with**: 5 target customers (via email, DM, or in-person)

**Question**: "I'm building [your hook]. Does this resonate? Would you try it?"

**Success**: 4/5 say yes + can repeat hook back to you (= sticky)

**Results**: ☐ Hook resonates ☐ Hook confusing ☐ Pivot hook

---

### Test 2: Influence Stack Testing (Week 2)

**Pitch**: Authority + Liking + Reciprocity stacked

"I'm an ADHD founder building tools for ADHD makers. 500+ beta users, 8/10 NPS. Here's a free 30-day challenge on [topic]. Curious?"

**Test with**: 10 prospects (email or social DM)

**Metrics**: Open rate, response rate, conversion to trial

**Success**: >30% response rate, >10% conversion to trial

**Results**: ☐ Stack works ☐ Adjust weapon order ☐ Try different weapons

---

### Test 3: Price Anchoring Testing (Week 3)

**Test group A** (shown high price first): See Enterprise ($500) → Pro ($99) → Starter ($50)

**Test group B** (shown low price first): See Starter ($50) → Pro ($99) → Enterprise ($500)

**Metric**: Which group chooses Pro tier? (Higher % = pricing order matters)

**Success**: Group A (high anchor first) converts to Pro at 2x higher rate

**Results**: ☐ Anchor works ☐ No difference ☐ Test more data

## Section 8: SUCCESS CRITERIA

**Offer Design Validated** when:

- ☐ Positioning statement passes "grandmother test" (non-technical person understands in 5 sec)
- ☐ Hero narrative resonates (customer sees themselves, nods)
- ☐ Sticky hook tested with 5 prospects → 4/5 remember + repeat it back
- ☐ Influence stack tested → 30%+ response rate on outreach
- ☐ Pricing anchor tested → High anchor first converts 2x better
- ☐ Objection scripts ready (10 objections × 3 responses, practiced)
- ☐ Ready for sales execution (Sales Closer agent can run system with scripts)

---

## Section 9: NEXT STEPS

**Day 31+**: Launch Sales Execution (Sales Closer agent → land first 10 customers).

**Metrics to track**:

- Conversion rate (% of prospects who sign up)
- Sales velocity (days to close)
- Deal size (average MRR per customer)
- Churn (% losing customers month-over-month)

**Pivot triggers**:

- Conversion <5% → Retest hook + influence stack
- Average deal size <$50/mo → Reprice or reposition
- Churn >10% → Product issue (not offer issue)
