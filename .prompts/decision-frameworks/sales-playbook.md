## Section 2: SALES PROCESS (7-Touch Sequence)

**Goal**: From prospect list to closed deal (14-21 days, high-intent prospects).

### Touch 1: Personalized Opening Email (Day 1)

**Goal**: Get response (not schedule, not pitch). Just conversation starter.

**Structure**:

- Line 1: Reference something specific about them (not generic)
- Line 2: Mention shared problem
- Line 3: Offer something free (reciprocity)
- CTA: "Quick call?"

**Example**:

> "Sarah — saw your company just hit 500 people (congrats!). Our founder managed ops at 300-person company, faced same Asana chaos you mentioned in [interview/article].
>
> We built simple task management for ops teams. Free 15-min audit of your current workflow?
>
> Available Tuesday 10am?"

**Your template**:

> ---

**Metrics**: Aim for 20-30% response rate (6-8 responses from 20 emails).

---

### Touch 2: Phone Call (Day 3, if response)

**Goal**: Qualify + build rapport + understand pain (NOT pitch).

**Structure** (15 minutes):

- 1 min: Rapport ("Hey Sarah, how's your week going?")
- 2 min: Permission ("Got 15 min? Appreciate it.")
- 3 min: Discovery ("What's the hardest part of task management right now?")
- 5 min: Positioning ("Here's how others solved it...")
- 3 min: Next step ("Make sense? Should we do 30-min demo together?")
- 1 min: Close ("Let me send calendar link")

**Discovery questions** (Fitzpatrick Mom Test):

- "What's hardest about your current tool?"
- "How are you solving this now?" (current workaround)
- "When was the last time you considered changing?" (timeline signal)
- "If you could fix ONE thing about Asana, what?" (problem priority)

**Red flags** (stop investing):

- "We're happy with [competitor]" + zero urgency = low intent
- Can't articulate problem = not suffering enough
- "I'd need to ask my boss" + unclear authority = gatekeeper (not decision maker)

**Green lights** (invest more):

- Specific pain ("20 hours/week on admin")
- Urgency ("Need to fix this month")
- Decision power ("I control $X budget")

**Next step language**:

> "Makes sense. Let me send calendar link for 30-min demo next Tuesday. We'll show you exactly how it works for ops teams."

---

### Touch 3: Demo / Meeting (Day 7)

**Goal**: Show product + position as solution to their specific pain + uncover objections.

**Structure** (30 minutes):

- 5 min: Their use case ("Walk me through typical week")
- 10 min: Live demo (show THEIR workflow, not generic demo)
- 10 min: Q&A
- 5 min: Next step ("Want to try 30-day trial?")

**Demo tips**:

- Show their pain point first (not features). "You mentioned 20 hrs/week on admin. Watch how we cut that to 10..."
- STOP talking about features. START showing outcomes. "Other ops teams save 10 hrs/week because of [feature], which means [outcome they care about]."
- Ask "Does that make sense?" → let them talk (don't over-explain)

**Objections that might come up**:

- "Looks good but we need to evaluate more" → Pivot: "Perfect, try free trial. Let's reconnect Friday?"
- "I need to talk to my team" → Pivot: "Make sense. Who else should I loop in?"
- "Too expensive" → Defer: "Let's get you in trial first, then we'll discuss pricing if it works."

**Close language**:

> "This can save your team 10 hours/week. How about we lock in 30-day trial, no credit card? You'll see results. Then let's talk pricing."

---

### Touch 4: Post-Demo Follow-Up (Day 10)

**Goal**: Keep momentum. Trial signup. Address lingering objections.

**Email**:

> "Sarah — Great discussion Friday. Few things we covered:
>
> 1. You need to cut task admin time
> 2. Simplicity > features (you said it!)
> 3. Your team's buy-in is key
>
> Here's what's next:
>
> - I've created your trial (link: [link])
> - Setup takes 30 min (I'll hop on if you want)
> - Try the focus mode (that's your pain point)
> - Let's reconnect Wed to see how it's going
>
> Sarah, I'm confident this solves your 20-hour problem. Let's validate it together."

**If no response after 2 days**: Call again. "Hi Sarah, not sure if email landed. Following up on trial access — want to jump on quick call?"

---

### Touch 5: Trial Usage Check-in (Day 14)

**Goal**: Get them using it. Uncover roadblocks. Build commitment.

**Call/message**:

> "Sarah — Quick check-in. Did you get a chance to try the trial?
>
> - How's setup going?
> - Used focus mode yet? (This is your main pain point)
> - Any blockers?
>
> I'm here to help. Let me know if you want a 15-min walkthrough."

**If they used it** (good sign):

- "What do you think? Saving time?" (let them sell themselves)
- "What could be better?" (objections to address)
- "Ready to commit to 30-day extended trial?" (micro-commitment)

**If they didn't use it** (bad sign):

- "What got in the way?"
- "Can we do quick 15-min walkthrough together?" (remove friction)
- If still no interest: "Not the right fit right now? Keep you in mind in 3 months?"

---

### Touch 6: Trial-to-Customer Negotiation (Day 18, if positive usage)

**Goal**: Close deal. Address pricing objections. Get commitment.

**Call/email**:

> "Sarah — You've been using it 5 days. Data shows:
>
> - 10 hours/week saved (vs your goal)
> - Team using it daily (good adoption signal)
>
> Let's talk next steps. Can you commit to 30-day paid plan?
>
> [Pricing options]:
>
> - $50/mo starter (your use case)
> - $99/mo pro (if team expands)
> - Custom if specific needs
>
> Which works?"

**Negotiation tactics** (Voss FBI):

- If "Too expensive": Mirror: "Too expensive?" → They explain → Calibrate: "What would work?" (don't counter-offer, make them suggest)
- If "Need to approve": Empathy: "Sounds like CFO approval is the blocker" → Calibrate: "What does CFO need to see?" (could be ROI doc, trial results, etc.)
- If "Need more time": Scarcity: "Early customer pricing is this month only. After that, it goes to $99/mo."

**Close language**:

> "Sarah, you've seen it works. Team's using it. Saving 10 hours/week. Let's do 30-day plan at $50/mo. Cancel anytime if it's not working. Deal?"

---

### Touch 7: Onboarding + Retention (Day 21, deal closed)

**Goal**: Successful onboarding = retention > revenue.

**Email**:

> "Sarah — Welcome! Thrilled to have Acme Corp on board.
>
> Here's what's next:
>
> 1. Onboarding call tomorrow 10am (30 min, walk team through setup)
> 2. I'll be your POC (point of contact)
> 3. 30-day check-in (make sure it's working)
>
> First goal: Hit 10 hrs/week savings by end of week 2."

**Follow-up** (Day 30):

> "Sarah — 30-day check-in. How's it going?
>
> Metrics from your account:
>
> - Team active 5/5 days ✓
> - Daily focus mode used ✓
> - 11 hours/week saved (beat goal!) ✓
>
> Love it. Let's extend to 3-month plan? Same $50/mo."
