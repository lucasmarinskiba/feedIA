# Action — Robert McKee & Bassim El-Wakil (2022)

**Authors**: Robert McKee (legendary story consultant), Bassim El-Wakil (screenwriter)  
**Core Concept**: "The Action Genre = struggle between hero/villain/victim over Life vs Death. Excitement = result of altruism at mercy of narcissism."

## Framework Central

**Action Core Value**: Life/Death (binary that drives all tension).

Action hero = selfless sacrifice for others. Action villain = selfish narcissism destroying lives. Action victim = helpless, can't save self.

**Core Emotion**: EXCITEMENT (not fear/terror). Hero's altruism keeps audience at "safe distance" even when stakes lethal.

**Action ≠ just violence**. Action = hero with altruistic spirit fighting villain with narcissistic lust for power.

## The Mercy Scene (Core Event of Action)

**Definition**: Moment hero se ve HELPLESS bajo poder villain. Villain can kill, pero delays gloating. Hero debe outsmart o overpower para escape.

**Why villain delays**: NARCISSISM. Vanity obliga gloating, torture, revelation de brilliant plan. Weakness Spinks explota.

### How Hero Turns Tables (2 Paths)

1. **Outsmart**: Exploit psychological blind spot del villain (arrogance = distraction vulnerability)
2. **Overpower**: Master villain's strength, use it against him (judo principle)

**Die Hard example**: McClane strips tied to radiator → baiting Hans into laugh → grab taped gun → shoot. Both outsmart + overpower.

## Hero/Villain/Victim Anatomy

### HEROISM: Spirit of Altruism

Hero = willingness to sacrifice own life para salvar otro (stranger, innocent).

- Superhero (Superman) vs Action hero (John McClane) vs Everyman hero (Captain Phillips)
- ALL express altruism extremo under pressure

**Arc**: Heroic character REVEALS true nature (doesn't change). Events expose who was siempre.

### VILLAINY: Spirit of Narcissism

Villain = indifference a humanity of victims. Pursues vision propia (scheme, power) regardless of collateral.

- Criminals commit crime, evade capture
- Psychopaths inflict torture for pleasure
- Villains ARE NOT Monsters (Horror realm): monsters are sadists who enjoy suffering. Villains just don't care.

### VICTIMHOOD: Spirit of Helplessness

Victim = can't save self (not cowardice, physical/circumstantial limitation). Well-drawn victim = symmetry: hero draws empathy (identifies as hero), victim draws sympathy (vulnerable).

## Action Subgenres (4 Types)

1. **Action-Adventure**: Hero vs natural forces, exotic locations, MacGuffin quest (Raiders of Lost Ark)
2. **Action-Epic**: Hero vs tyranny/empire (Star Wars, Braveheart)
3. **Action-Duel**: Hero vs villain one-on-one moral combat (Terminator 2, The Dark Knight)
4. **Action-Thriller**: Hero vs psychopathic villain with visceral stakes (Die Hard, Mission: Impossible)

## Excitement (Core Emotion) — 5 Sources

1. **Rebellion vs Authority**: Protagonist vs power structure
2. **Exploring Unknown**: Fear of unseen → safe-distance excitement
3. **Coping with Frustration**: Goal nearly in reach, barriers block
4. **Conquering Limitations**: Physical/temporal constraints, time ticking
5. **Breaking Taboos**: Violating sacred norms

## Anti-Patterns (Action Failures)

- ❌ **Absent Villain**: Henchmen face-offs flatten mercy scene (no narcissism battle)
- ❌ **Obvious Achilles Heel**: Audience predicts weakness > loses impact
- ❌ **Clichéd Distractions**: "Look behind you" / "Your shoelace untied"
- ❌ **Coincidence Saves**: Random luck (car backfire) vs earned hero cleverness
- ❌ **Deus Ex Machina**: Last-minute rescue by unrelated force (partner arrives, cops save) = removes hero agency

## Prompt Activación (Story/Video Creation)

```
"Apply Action framework:
- Core value: Life vs Death threat must be PRESENT and ESCALATING
- Cast: Hero (altruistic spirit) vs Villain (narcissistic self-serving) vs Victim (helpless)
- Mercy scene: Hero cornered, defenseless, villain gloating → hero outsmarts OR overpowers (no coincidence)
- Excitement source: Rebellion/exploration/frustration/limitation/taboo (which drives THIS story?)
- Subgenre: Adventure/Epic/Duel/Thriller? (affects pacing, villain psychology)
- Hero arc: REVEAL true nature (altruism under fire), not CHANGE character
- Villain motivation: Scheme/vision so important victim's death is acceptable collateral
- Avoid: Henchmen mercy scenes, obvious flaws, deus ex machina
- Measure: Do audiences feel excitement (safe distance danger) not terror?"
```
