# Sales System Full (Cross-Book Framework)

**Core**: Sales = system, not inspiration. 12 core strategies executed 4,000 times > 4,000 tactics once. FBI tactics unlock objections. Influence weapons stack. Ideas stick.

## Components

### 1. 12 KEY STRATEGIES (Holmes — Ultimate Sales Machine)

1. **Time Management** — Energy peaks. Call at 9am, not 5pm.
2. **Standards & Training** — Script every scenario. No winging.
3. **Effective Meetings** — Structure: Agenda, owner, time limits.
4. **Strategy** — 9x impact via focus (not brute force).
5. **Hiring** — Superstars only (multiplier effect).
6. **Fast, Cheap Acquisition** — Fastest, least expensive customer path.
7. **Marketing Fundamentals** — 7 musts (apply always).
8. **Compelling Visuals** — No death-by-PowerPoint.
9. **Day-by-Day Tactics** — Specifics to land dream clients.
10. **Sales Skills** — Objection handling. Follow-up.
11. **Follow-up & Bonding** — Keep clients forever (loyalty = profit).
12. **Systems & Goals** — Measurement, activation, execution.

**Principle**: Pigheaded discipline. 12 moves × 4,000 times = automatic excellence.

### 2. FBI NEGOTIATION TACTICS (Voss — Never Split the Difference)

- **Mirror**: Repeat last 3 words. Makes them explain deeper.
- **Tactical Empathy**: Label emotions (not pity). "It sounds like X matters."
- **Master "No"**: Safe space to say no. "What would work instead?"
- **Trigger "That's Right"**: Summarize their position. When they say "that's right" = real agreement.
- **Calibrated Questions**: "How am I supposed to do that?" "What would you need?" Makes them solve WITH you.
- **Black Swan**: Unknown unknowns. Hidden constraints. When found = unblocks everything.

**Principle**: Emotion > logic. Listen 70%, talk 30%.

### 3. 6 INFLUENCE WEAPONS (Cialdini — Influence)

- **Reciprocity**: Give first. Creates obligation.
- **Commitment**: Small asks → escalate.
- **Social Proof**: "Gotta like us do things like this."
- **Authority**: Specific credentials (10 years, Dr., titles).
- **Liking**: Similarity, compliments, shared goals.
- **Scarcity**: Real/perceived limited availability.

**Stacking**: Combine 3-4 weapons per pitch. Combined > single.

### 4. STICKY IDEAS (Heath — Made to Stick)

**SUCCESs**:

- **S**imple: Core + compact.
- **U**nexpected: Break pattern.
- **C**oncrete: Specific details (not abstract).
- **C**redible: Testable. Specific numbers (27g > "lots").
- **E**motional: Identifiable victim (not statistics).
- **S**tories: Challenge/Connection/Creativity plots.

**Principle**: Complex idea? Use story as Trojan horse.

## Example Build

**Scenario**: B2B SaaS sales (selling $5K/mo software).

1. **System Setup** (Holmes):
   - Strategy 1: Morning calls only (9-11am = highest energy).
   - Strategy 2: Script for 10 objections (prep not wing).
   - Strategy 3: 3 touchpoint follow-up (email → call → email).
   - Strategy 10: Objection playbook (each with 3 responses).
   - Strategy 11: Quarterly check-in (bonding call, no sell).

2. **Prospect Research** (Holmes):
   - Know: Their revenue, team size, growth, top 3 competitors.
   - Pain point: "Manual reporting taking 20 hours/week."
   - Ideal outcome: "Automate reporting, free up team time."

3. **Opening** (Cialdini):
   - Authority: "We work with 500+ companies in your industry." (credential)
   - Liking: "You mentioned you're hiring aggressively — we help with that." (similarity)
   - Reciprocity: "Free 15-min audit of your current process (value first)."

4. **Pitch** (Heath):
   - Simple: "Automate your reporting in 1 week."
   - Unexpected: "Save 20 hours/week (their exact pain point)."
   - Concrete: "Setup takes 4 hours. Auto-run thereafter. $5K/mo saves $40K/month in labor."
   - Credible: "27 companies using this exact template."
   - Emotional: "One client: 'Gave me back 5 hours/week with family.'"
   - Story: Case study. Customer took role, built dashboard, discovered issue. Now proactive.

5. **Objection Handling** (Holmes):
   - Common: "Too expensive."
   - Response 1: "What would be a comfortable investment?" (calibrated question).
   - Response 2: "Saves $40K/month. You'll ROI in 7 days." (math)
   - Response 3: "Payment plan available. Can start $2.5K/mo." (offer flexibility)

6. **Negotiation** (Voss):
   - Prospect: "That's expensive."
   - Mirror: "Expensive?" (repeat last word)
   - Prospect explains deeper: "Yeah, budget frozen."
   - Empathy: "It sounds like budget is your constraint." (label)
   - Calibrate: "What would you need to unlock budget?" (make them solve)
   - Black Swan: "Is it budget, or is it approval from CFO that's the blocker?" (unearth hidden constraint)

7. **Commitment** (Cialdini + Voss):
   - Get "that's right": Summarize their position. "So you want to automate, budget locked, need CFO sign-off?"
   - Prospect: "That's right." (real agreement).
   - Escalate: "Let's loop in CFO Thursday. I'll show ROI." (small escalation)

8. **Follow-up** (Holmes):
   - Day 0: Send pitch deck (email).
   - Day 2: "You mentioned Tuesday meeting — confirmed?" (call).
   - Day 5 (post-meeting): "Great discussion. Next step: CFO email." (email).
   - Day 10 (contract signed): "Thank you. Onboarding starts Monday. I'll be your POC." (bonding)
   - Q2 (ongoing): Quarterly check-in call (no sell, just value).
