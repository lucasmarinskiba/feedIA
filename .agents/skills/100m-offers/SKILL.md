# Alex Hormozi — $100M Offers (2021)

**Core**: Grand Slam Offer = combination of pricing + value + guarantees + scarcity + urgency + naming that makes people feel stupid saying no.

**Baseball Metaphor**:

- Singles/Doubles = keep game going, pay bills
- Grand Slam = score 4 runs at once. In business = 1000x payoff
- Takes same effort to strike out vs grand slam. Difference = skill.

## Value Equation (Core Framework)

```
Value = (Perceived Value - Perceived Price) / Time Delay

↑ Perceived Value = Make offer look better
↑ Perceived Price = Position as premium (not discount)
↓ Perceived Price = Remove costs (hidden fees)
↓ Time Delay = Deliver faster, urgency
```

**To increase value:**

1. Increase perceived value (bundle, improve, add social proof)
2. Decrease perceived price (payment plans, remove friction)
3. Reduce time delay (instant access, fast delivery)

## Grand Slam Offer Components

### 1. PRICING

- **Problem**: Most entrepreneurs underprice (feels guilty raising price)
- **Solution**: Charge what solves customer's problem worth to them
- **Starving Crowd**: Find market that wants solution + has money
- **Anchor High**: Show premium price first, then your price seems reasonable

### 2. VALUE STACK

- Core offer + Bonuses + Guarantees = "stack" total value
- Don't just lower price. Add layers of value perceived > actual cost
- Example: Core course ($2K) + templates ($500 value) + coaching ($5K value) = $7.5K perceived but costs you $300 to deliver

### 3. GUARANTEES

- **Risk reversal**: You take the risk, not customer
- Types: Money-back, satisfaction, performance (specific outcome)
- Removes objection "What if it doesn't work?"

### 4. SCARCITY

- Real (limited spots, deadline, inventory)
- Never fake. People detect + lose trust
- "Last 10 spots" > "only 10 spots" (implies scarcity ending soon)

### 5. URGENCY

- Artificial deadline (launch ends Friday)
- Reason required (bonus expires, price goes up, limited availability)
- Create reason before launch, don't fabricate

### 6. NAMING

- Name positions offer in prospect's mind
- "Gym Launch" (what it does) > "personal training program"
- Names trigger associations + make it memorable

## Offer Design Process

1. **Identify Starving Crowd** — Who wants this + has money?
2. **Define Value Equation** — What's worth to them?
3. **Price Confidently** — Charge for value, not hours
4. **Stack Value** — Bonus layers (templates, templates, community, etc.)
5. **Add Guarantee** — Risk reversal
6. **Create Scarcity** — Real constraint (limited spots)
7. **Set Urgency** — Deadline + reason
8. **Name Memorably** — Positions offer in mind

## Case Study: Fitness Offers

**Before**: Generic "fitness coaching" = $99/month = race to bottom

**After (Grand Slam)**:

- Core: Weight loss program ($5K perceived value)
- Bonus: Custom meal plans ($2K perceived value)
- Bonus: Community coaching calls ($3K perceived value)
- Guarantee: 20 lbs lost in 90 days or money back
- Scarcity: 20 spots max (fills first 3 days)
- Urgency: Launch price $997, regular $2,997 (price goes up after)
- Name: "90-Day Body Transformation Challenge"

**Result**: Higher price + perceived higher value + lower refund rate + higher customer success (they're invested)

## Anti-Patterns

- ❌ Underpricing (guilt about charging)
- ❌ No value stack (just core offer)
- ❌ Fake scarcity/urgency (destroys trust)
- ❌ No guarantee (high objection rate)
- ❌ Generic naming (forgettable)
- ❌ Wrong starving crowd (mismatched market)
