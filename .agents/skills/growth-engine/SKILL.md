# Growth Engine (Cross-Book Framework)

**Core**: Growth = systematic funnel through DEAL phases + Bullseye channel selection + Psychological priming + Viral amplification.

## Components

### 1. DEAL FRAMEWORK (Ferriss — 4-Hour Workweek)

**Phase 1: DEFINITION** — What growth target? Specific, not vague.

- Example: "100 customers in 90 days" (not "get more users").
- Fear-setting: Worst case if fail? Survivable.

**Phase 2: ELIMINATION** — 80/20 ruthless. Cut waste.

- Parkinson's Law: Compress timeline → same output 1/4 time.
- Batch interruptions (email 2x/day, not constant).
- Eliminate low-ROI channels early.

**Phase 3: AUTOMATION** — Scale what works.

- Outsource repetitive ($5-15/hr).
- Income autopilot (passive leads, referral loops).
- Detach effort from output.

**Phase 4: LIBERATION** — Sustain without manual work.

- Systems run without founder.
- Distributed leads (not single channel dependent).
- Mini-retirements possible (proof of automation).

### 2. 19 TRACTION CHANNELS + BULLSEYE (Weinberg — Traction)

**Channels**: Viral, PR, Unconventional PR, SEO, Content, Speaking, Community, SEM, Social Ads, Offline Ads, Engineering as Marketing, Targeting Blogs, Existing Platforms, BD, Sales, Affiliates, Trade Shows, Offline Events, Email.

**Bullseye Framework**:

- Outer ring: Brainstorm all 19 (consider all).
- Middle ring: Rank by effort/reach/confidence.
- Inner ring: Prioritize top 3-5.
- Test: 2-week sprints each.
- Focus: Winner. Ignore rest.

**Key insight**: One channel dominates. Test, don't predict.

### 3. RELATIVITY ANCHOR (Ariely — Predictably Irrational)

- All decisions relative to anchor.
- **First anchor wins**: Show premium offer/price first → everything else cheap.
- Zero-cost effect: FREE activates loss aversion (trial → conversion).
- Ownership bias: Customer invested = overvalue = retention.

**Application**:

- Anchor high (premium tier shown first).
- Free tier/trial (loss aversion).
- Paywall (customers invested = stick).

### 4. VIRAL MECHANICS (Berger — Contagious)

**STEPPS**:

- **S**ocial Currency: Share what makes us look good.
- **T**riggers: Link to frequent cues (coffee, email, morning).
- **E**motion: High arousal (awe, ire, excitement). NOT low (sadness, contentment).
- **P**ublic: Behavioral residue visible (others see you use it).
- **P**ractical Value: Info useful → shareable.
- **S**tories: Trojan horse (product inseparable from story).

**93% WOM offline**: Design for conversation, not just social media.

## Example Build

**Goal**: 100 SaaS customers in 90 days.

1. **Definition** (Ferriss):
   - Target: 100 paying customers (not vanity metric).
   - Fear-setting: Worst case if fail = startup dies. Probability = 20%. Acceptable?
   - Timeline: 90 days (compressed vs "open-ended").

2. **Elimination** (Ferriss):
   - Cut: Product features not on critical path (delay to post-launch).
   - Cut: Founders' time on non-customer tasks (delegate).
   - Batch: Email checking 2x/day (recover 10 hours/week).
   - Timeline compression: MVP in 30 days (vs 90 days typical).

3. **Bullseye** (Weinberg):
   - Test 19 channels (2-week sprint each): Content marketing (SEO), Community (Slack groups), SEM (Google Ads), Affiliates (partner referrals), Email (cold outreach).
   - Rank by reach/effort/confidence: Content + Affiliates = high reach, low effort. Likely winners.
   - Prioritize top 3: Content (blog posts) + Community (engage Slack) + Email (outreach).

4. **Anchor Psychology** (Ariely):
   - Show $999/mo "Enterprise" tier first (anchor high).
   - Reveal $99/mo "Starter" (seems cheap).
   - Free 14-day trial (loss aversion: already time-invested after 3 days).
   - Paywall: Paid tier after trial (customers invested = stick).

5. **STEPPS Virality** (Berger):
   - Social Currency: "Join 500+ SaaS founders" (status of users).
   - Triggers: Email reminders (frequent, tied to morning routine).
   - Emotion: "Never miss a lead again" (anxiety relief = high arousal).
   - Public: Dashboard visible (users see you're active → demo to colleagues).
   - Practical: Lead scoring template (useful, share with team).
   - Stories: Case study of founder 10x-ing leads (trojan horse product reveal).

6. **Automation** (Ferriss):
   - Outsource: Email follow-up sequences (VA $10/hr).
   - Autopilot: Referral loop (customers refer → get credit).
   - Systemize: Content calendar (batch 4 weeks at once).

7. **Liberation** (Ferriss):
   - Founder not in loop: Email sequences run solo.
   - Referral loop runs solo.
   - Content published via scheduler.
   - Result: 100 customers acquired with founder 10 hours/week.
