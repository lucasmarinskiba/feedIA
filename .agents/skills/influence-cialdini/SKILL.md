# Robert Cialdini — Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion (1984/1993)

**Core**: Humans have fixed-action patterns triggered by specific cues, like turkeys responding to "tchip-tchip". These triggers override rational thinking. Automatic compliance.

**Guiding Principle**: "Click-whirr" = automatic response to trigger (like tape cassette activating). Humans are automatic AND rational, depending on context.

## 6 Weapons of Influence

### 1. RECIPROCITY

- Giving = obligation to return.
- Give first (lead magnet, free sample, value).
- Creates "debt" (felt obligation).
- Disproportionate: small gift → large return.

### 2. COMMITMENT & CONSISTENCY

- Small commitments → escalating commitments.
- "Foot in the door" (yes to small ask).
- Consistency drive: humans want to appear consistent.
- Written commitment > verbal (more binding).

### 3. SOCIAL PROOF

- "People like us do things like this."
- Testimonials from similar people > expert authority.
- Uncertainty = look to others for cues.
- Real-time decisions: what are others doing?

### 4. AUTHORITY

- Titles, credentials, uniforms trigger compliance.
- Specific numbers (Dr. with 10 years experience) > general authority.
- Third-party authority (as seen in, featured in) > self-proclamation.
- Appearance of authority sufficient (uniform, title).

### 5. LIKING

- We comply with people we like.
- Similarity (we like people like us).
- Compliments (we like people who praise us).
- Cooperation toward shared goal (creates bond).

### 6. SCARCITY

- Rare = valuable (artificial or real).
- "Only 3 left" > "plenty available".
- Exclusivity ("VIP only") > mass availability.
- Loss frame stronger than gain frame.

## Fixed-Action Pattern Example

**Turkey experiment** (M.W. Fox):

- Turkey attacks weasel (natural enemy).
- Turkey mothers baby turkeys when they emit "tchip-tchip" sound.
- Weasel puppet WITHOUT sound → turkey attacks.
- Weasel puppet WITH "tchip-tchip" recording → turkey adopts weasel.
- Stop recording → turkey attacks again.

**The trigger**: "Tchip-tchip" sound (not actual baby turkey). Automatic response bypasses reason.

**Human parallel** (Ellen Langer study):

- "Can I cut in line?" → 60% yes.
- "Can I cut in line because I'm in a rush?" → 94% yes.
- "Can I cut in line because I need to make copies?" (no real reason) → 93% yes.

**The trigger**: "Because" (word itself activates automatic compliance, even with no real reason).

## Auto-Compliance Triggers

- FREE (zero-cost effect, Ariely applies)
- SCARCE (limited availability)
- AUTHORITY (title, credentials)
- SOCIAL PROOF (others doing it)
- LIKING (similarity, compliments)
- RECIPROCITY (debt from gift)
- CONSISTENCY (commitment escalation)

## Anti-Patterns

- ❌ Authority without specificity (unbelievable)
- ❌ Fake scarcity (destroys trust)
- ❌ No social proof in decision-heavy context
- ❌ Large first ask (no foot-in-door escalation)
- ❌ Generalist liking (not targeted similarity)
