# Robert McKee — Story (1997)

**Core Philosophy**: Not rules but principles. Not formulas but eternal universal forms. Mastery requires understanding craft deeply before innovation.

## Story vs Screenplay

- Story = universal principles of narrative form
- Screenplay = specific medium (film, TV, etc.)
- Principles apply across all storytelling mediums

## Key Distinctions

### Principles vs Rules

- **Rule**: "You must do it this way" (prescriptive)
- **Principle**: "This works, always has" (descriptive of what works)
- Anxious writers obey rules. Rebels break them. Artists master form.

### Archetypes vs Stereotypes

- **Archetype** — Universal human experience expressed in culturally specific detail (travels across cultures, endures across generations)
  - Example: Como agua para chocolate — Universally recognizable mother/daughter conflict (dependence vs independence) dressed in rich Spanish/Mexican specificity
- **Stereotype** — Narrow cultural specificity (stays at home, doesn't travel)
  - Generic, dated, vague, uninspired

**Key insight**: "Stereotyped stories stay at home. Archetypal stories travel." Add specific, original details to universal conflicts.

### Forms vs Formulas

- **Form** — Eternal patterns underlying all great storytelling (across 23 centuries since Aristotle)
- **Formula** — Guaranteed commercial recipes (don't exist; flops use same "formulas" as hits)
- No Hollywood prototype. Variance within form.

## Storytelling Fundamentals

### The Writer's 10 Faculties

To master story, develop:

1. **Taste** — Disgust with banality, eye for the vital
2. **Knowledge** — Observe, research, read, travel, experience
3. **Originality** — Unearth hidden truths, combine influences uniquely
4. **Showmanship** — Entertain AND reveal
5. **Awareness of Reader/Audience** — Moment-by-moment impact
6. **Mastery of Form** — Story structure first, then innovation
7. **Hatred of Clichés** — Know history, then break it deliberately
8. **Moral Imagination** — Understand human binaries deeply (good/evil, love/hate, order/chaos)
9. **Ideal Self** — Write as your best self, not defensive self
10. **Self-Knowledge** — All character creation begins/ends in own humanity

### The Camera as Truth Machine

**Terrifying truth**: Film is "X-ray machine that reveals lies."

- Novelist can hide behind prose
- Playwright can hide behind monologue
- Screenwriter has NOWHERE to hide
- Every weak turn, confused motivation, colorless emotion magnified onscreen
- Technique cannot mask falsehood

**Implication**: You must understand your story deeply. Surface-level thinking fails on film.

## Core Principles

### Originality = Form + Content Union

- **Content** (what you say) — Ambientazione, personaggi, idee
- **Form** (how you say it) — Selezione e organizzazione degli eventi
- Original vision = original form + original content, inseparable
- Style = personal cosmology (how you see world, why things happen)

**Clichéd content** → Clichéd form. **Clichéd form** → Stereotyped characters/settings needed.
**Original vision** → Original form AND original content, reinforcing each other.

### Respect for Audience

- Audience IQ rises 25 points in dark theater (more intelligent than film they watch)
- Audience does NOT defend against emotions (vulnerable, receptive)
- Audience is emotionally open but intellectually sharp
- Screenplay must satisfy BOTH: express your vision AND satisfy audience desires
- Screenwriter's job: stay one step ahead of concentrated audience

### Mastery Before Breaking

- Do NOT sell first drafts chasing commercial trends
- Write, rewrite patiently until work is mature, refined
- Incomplete work invites meddling. Complete work ensures integrity.
- Unoriginal + hastily-written copy joins thousands of mediocre scripts in Hollywood

**Path to success**: Master craft → Write brilliant original screenplay → Agent sells it → Producers, directors, actors recognize good writing = depend on it for careers

## Anti-Patterns

- ❌ Eccentricity mistaken for originality (different just to be different)
- ❌ "Auteur" tricks (drawing attention to self, not story)
- ❌ Childish rebellion against conventions without understanding them first
- ❌ Formula worship (trying to replicate last summer's hit)
- ❌ Contempt for audience (underestimating intelligence)
- ❌ Shortcuts before mastery (selling incomplete work, writing quickly)
