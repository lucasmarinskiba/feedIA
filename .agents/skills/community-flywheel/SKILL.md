# Community Flywheel (Cross-Book Framework)

**Core**: Community = self-sustaining flywheel via distributed leadership, member-delivered value (SPACES), systematic growth stages, and relationship authenticity.

## Components

### 1. FLYWHEEL MOMENTUM (Collins — Good to Great)

**Concept**: Consistent push → moment builds → exponential acceleration. No single breakthrough.

**Pattern**:

- Turn 1: Small push (50 members).
- Turn 2: Members create value (events, guides).
- Turn 3: New members attracted (momentum visible).
- Turn 4: Breakeven (self-sustaining, no founder push needed).
- Turn ∞: Exponential (each turn = 2x output).

**Anti-pattern**: Doom Loop (reactive, chaotic, no consistent push → exhaustion).

**Key**: Consistency > amplitude. 12 months steady = exponential. 1 month intense = zero.

### 2. SPACES OUTCOMES (Spinks — Business of Belonging)

Members get value in 6 categories:

- **S**upport: Member → Member help (Q&A, mentoring, peer support).
- **P**roduct: Community influences product. Feedback loop.
- **A**cquisition: WOM, referrals. Community attracts non-members.
- **C**ontribution: UGC, ideas, case studies. Members build with brand.
- **E**ngagement: Retention, loyalty, return rate. Sticky members.
- **S**uccess: Education, growth. Members improve via community.

**Application**: Audit SPACES. Which 2-3 underserved? Invest there.

### 3. 3 STAGES + 9 STEPS (Richardson — Get Together)

**Stage 1: SPARK**

- (1) Pinpoint people (WHO shares purpose, WHY).
- (2) Do something together (activity = condition community exists).
- (3) Get people talking (horizontal conversation, not vertical to leader).

**Stage 2: STOKE**

- (4) Attract new (passionate people, not everyone).
- (5) Cultivate identity (name, rituals, values, history = "I am part of X").
- (6) Pay attention (observe who keeps showing up = future leaders).

**Stage 3: PASS**

- (7) Create more leaders (question every challenge: WITH not FOR?).
- (8) Supercharge (tools, training, visibility, autonomy).
- (9) Celebrate (individual + collective wins = identity consolidation).

### 4. GENUINE APPRECIATION (Carnegie — Win Friends)

**Principle**: Don't criticize. Give specific appreciation. Awaken their desire.

**Application to community**:

- Recognize members publicly (specific achievement, not generic praise).
- Ask questions instead of commands (Socratic: let them conclude).
- Make them feel important (show their contribution matters).
- Listen more than you talk (4 ears vs 1 mouth).

**Lock-in**: Members who feel genuinely appreciated = become ambassadors.

## Example Build

**Community**: Indie hackers community (SaaS founders).

1. **Spark** (Richardson):
   - WHO: Founders building solo (shared identity).
   - WHY: Build in public + get feedback + find co-founders.
   - DO: Weekly Zoom calls (ship what you built this week).
   - Horizontal: Peer-to-peer (not founder lecturing).

2. **Appreciation** (Carnegie):
   - Recognize member launches publicly (Slack, email): "@Sarah shipped [product] in 2 weeks. Insane."
   - Ask: "How did you find co-founder?" (let them teach others).
   - Show impact: "Your advice helped 3 members launch."

3. **Stoke** (Richardson):
   - Attract: Invite passionate builders (not everyone).
   - Identity: "Indie Hackers India" (name, logo, monthly rituals).
   - Observe: Ravi answers 10 questions/week → future leader.

4. **SPACES Value** (Spinks):
   - Support: Peer help threads (members teach members).
   - Product: Members request features, we build.
   - Acquisition: "Tell a friend" referral (WOM).
   - Contribution: Case studies (members write about their product).
   - Engagement: 70% retention (weekly calls sticky).
   - Success: Members average $5K MRR (community education).

5. **Identify Leaders** (Richardson):
   - Ravi (helper), Priya (organizer), Anmol (speaker).
   - They keep showing up. They have status in group.

6. **Pass Leadership** (Richardson):
   - Ravi: "Lead Q&A thread Fridays?" (autonomy).
   - Priya: "Organize monthly in-person meetup?" (visibility).
   - Anmol: "Record case study video?" (tools/training).
   - Each: Public credit. "Thanks to leaders..."

7. **Flywheel Momentum** (Collins):
   - Turn 1: 50 members (founder pushing).
   - Turn 2: Leaders creating content. Momentum visible.
   - Turn 3: New members attracted (WOM).
   - Turn 4: Self-sustaining (leaders drive, founder facilitates).
   - Turn ∞: 1000 members. 5 sub-leaders. Exponential.
