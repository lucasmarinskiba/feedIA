## 4 Core Ways to Get Leads

### 1. WARM OUTREACH

- **Definition**: Contact people you/your network know
- **Method**: Email, text, call, DM existing contacts
- **Advantage**: High trust, high conversion, low cost
- **Disadvantage**: Limited scale (finite network)
- **Use case**: Early stage, B2B, relationship-based

### 2. POST FREE CONTENT

- **Definition**: Create valuable content (blog, video, social, podcast)
- **Method**: Platform-native storytelling. Jab then right hook.
- **Advantage**: Long-term compounding, owned audience
- **Disadvantage**: Slow to start, requires consistency
- **Use case**: Authority building, SEO, community, brand
- **Note**: "Free Goodwill" = give massive value before monetizing

### 3. COLD OUTREACH

- **Definition**: Contact strangers (email, phone, direct mail, DM)
- **Method**: Scaled one-to-one at volume
- **Advantage**: Predictable, scalable, fast
- **Disadvantage**: Lower conversion than warm, requires testing
- **Use case**: B2B, high-ticket, sales-driven
- **Key**: Personalization > template increases response 10x

### 4. RUN PAID ADS

- **Definition**: Pay platform (Google, Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.) to show offer
- **Method**: Ads target specific audience, drive to offer/landing page
- **Advantage**: Fastest scaling, predictable (with data), largest audience
- **Disadvantage**: Requires capital, learning curve, ongoing cost
- **Use case**: Viral potential, mass market, repeatable offers
- **Foundation**: Must have good offer first (otherwise burn cash)

## Get Lead Getters (Leverage)

Don't do it yourself. Get others to get leads for you:

### 1. CUSTOMER REFERRALS (Word of Mouth)

- **Method**: Incentivize existing customers to refer friends
- **Advantage**: Highest trust, lowest churn, word-of-mouth power
- **Disadvantage**: Slow to build momentum
- **Structure**: Bonus for each referral (money, discount, exclusive)

### 2. EMPLOYEES (Sales/Marketing Team)

- **Method**: Hire people whose job is to get leads
- **Advantage**: Scalable, consistent, full-time focus
- **Disadvantage**: Payroll, management, training
- **Structure**: Commission + salary. Performance-based

### 3. AGENCIES

- **Method**: Hire external marketing agency to run ads/content
- **Advantage**: Hands-off, experts handle it
- **Disadvantage**: Cost (30-50% of revenue typical), less control
- **Structure**: Contract for specific results or retainer

### 4. AFFILIATES & PARTNERS

- **Method**: Pay commissions to partners who bring customers
- **Advantage**: Scalable, no fixed cost, risk-free
- **Disadvantage**: Loss of control, quality varies
- **Structure**: Commission only. Performance-based.

## Three Levels of Execution

1. **More** — Do current channel more (more ads spend, more outreach calls)
2. **Better** — Improve conversion rate (better copy, better landing page)
3. **New** — Add new channel (if doing ads, add cold outreach; add referral program)

**Order matters**: Better (improve existing) > New (add channel) > More (more spend). ROI highest when improving before scaling.

## Anti-Patterns

- ❌ No offer ready (ads waste money on bad offers)
- ❌ Only one lead channel (over-reliant, no backup)
- ❌ Paid ads only (scaling unsustainable without warm/content/referral base)
- ❌ Generic ads (no audience targeting, low conversion)
- ❌ No tracking (don't know which channel works)
