# Chip & Dan Heath — Made to Stick (2007)

**Framework**: SUCCESs — 6 principles for ideas that stick (memorable + understandable + effective in changing thought/behavior).

## Core Principles

### 1. SIMPLE

- **Core + Compact** — Extract core of idea. Strip away excess.
- **Commander's Intent** — Military: mission clarity without micromanagement
- **Inverted Pyramid** — Lead with conclusion, not buried at end
- **Proverbs** — Timeless, compact wisdom (universal truths in minimal words)
- **High Concept** — "Jaws on a spaceship" (Alien). Combines known ideas in novel way
- **Schema** — Activate existing mental models (pomelo = apple + grapefruit)
- **Generative Analogies** — Disney "cast members" (not employees) shifts mindset

**Anti-pattern**: Over-explanation. Too many details. Buried leads.

### 2. UNEXPECTED

- **Break the Guessing Machine** — Violate expectations to wake people up
- **Gap Theory of Curiosity** — Recognize gap between what people know + want to know
- **Priming the Gap** — Create itch, then scratch it
- **The Surprise Brow** — Attention-getting moment before payoff
- **"The Nordie"** — Personal, specific stories stick more than data

**Anti-pattern**: Gimmicky surprise without substance. Postdictability (obvious in hindsight).

### 3. CONCRETE

- **Velcro Theory of Memory** — Concrete details stick (hooks to existing memory)
- **Less Abstraction** — Teach subtraction with objects, not equations
- **Brown Eyes, Blue Eyes** — Personal, observed examples > statistics
- **The Leather Computer** — Brand names + specific imagery stick
- **Oral Rehydration Therapy Case** — Simple, concrete solution beats jargon

**Anti-pattern**: Abstract concepts without concrete examples. Vague language.

### 4. CREDIBLE

- **Powerful Details** — One specific number (27 grams) > vague ("lots")
- **Testable Credentials** — Expert can back up claims
- **Authority + Anti-Authority** — Expert can be traditional OR unconventional
- **The Sinatra Test** — "If I can make it there, I can make it anywhere" (proof)
- **Human-Scale Principle** — "Our offices = soccer team" (scale) > "50M employees"
- **Statistics: Nuclear Warheads as BBs** — Translate big numbers to human scale

**Anti-pattern**: Authority without specificity. Unsubstantiated claims.

### 5. EMOTIONAL

- **If I look at the one, I will act** — Identifiable victim > abstract statistics
- **Mother Teresa Principle** — Personalization creates emotional response
- **WIIFY** — "What's In It For You?" — Appeal to audience's values, not yours
- **Avoiding Maslow's Basement** — Appeal to aspirations, not basic needs
- **Semantic Stretch** — Don't say "unique" (diluted); find precise emotion word

**Anti-pattern**: Abstract suffering. No personal connection. Generic appeals.

### 6. STORIES

- **Shop Talk at Xerox** — Real customer stories as flight simulators
- **3 Plot Types**:
  1. Challenge Plot — Hero faces obstacle, overcomes through ingenuity
  2. Connection Plot — People come together despite differences
  3. Creativity Plot — Person uses resources in novel way
- **Jared, the 425-pound dieter** — Inspiring story (specific transformation)
- **Springboard Stories** — World Bank health worker story in Zambia activates thinking

**Anti-pattern**: Generic story. No clear plot. Doesn't inspire action.

## Cross-Principles

**SUCCESs Stack**: All 6 together = maximum stickiness.

- Simple (idea accessible)
- Unexpected (gets attention)
- Concrete (specific details stick)
- Credible (believable)
- Emotional (resonates)
- Stories (memorable + actionable)

## Case Study: Movie Popcorn

**Problem**: 37 grams saturated fat in popcorn. How to communicate?

- ❌ "37 grams of saturated fat" = numbers glaze over eyes
- ❌ Bar graph = rational, not impactful
- ✅ "Bacon-eggs breakfast + Big Mac + fries + steak dinner = 1 bag popcorn"

**Result**: Simple (core message) + Unexpected (shocking) + Concrete (specific comparison) + Credible (lab data) + Emotional (disgust) = Immediate behavior change. Theater chains dropped coconut oil.

## Activation for Agents

```
"Apply SUCCESs framework to all content:
SIMPLE: Core message first. Strip non-essential. Use generative analogies (X + Y = Z).
UNEXPECTED: Break pattern. Create curiosity gap. Prime the gap before reveal.
CONCRETE: Specific details over vague claims. Velcro principle (hooks to memory).
CREDIBLE: Testable details. Human-scale numbers. Authority with specificity.
EMOTIONAL: Identifiable person not statistics. WIIFY (audience values). Inspiration over fear.
STORIES: Challenge/Connection/Creativity plot. Springboard stories activate thinking.
Anti-patterns: Vague abstractions, unsubstantiated claims, generic stories, appeals to basic needs instead of aspirations."
```
