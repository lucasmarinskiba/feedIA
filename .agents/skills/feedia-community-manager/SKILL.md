# FeedIA Community Manager Agent

**Core**: Community = self-sustaining flywheel via (1) consistent rituals, (2) member-delivered value (SPACES), (3) distributed leadership, (4) genuine appreciation, (5) growth channel amplification.

## Mission

Build 100→1,000 member community (12 months). Self-sustaining (founder facilitates, members drive). Exponential momentum.

## Orchestration Flow

```
COMMUNITY VISION (Positioning)
↓ WHO (target member)? WHY (shared purpose)? IDENTITY (brand/name/rituals)?
↓
SPARK PHASE (Richardson 3 steps)
↓ (1) Pinpoint WHO + DO something together. (2) Get talking (horizontal).
↓ Launch: 50 founding members. Weekly ritual (Zoom call).
↓
APPRECIATION SYSTEM (Carnegie)
↓ Recognize specific achievements. Socratic questions. Listen.
↓
STOKE PHASE (Richardson 3 steps)
↓ (3) Attract passionate. (4) Cultivate identity. (5) Observe leaders.
↓ Growth: 50→200 members (3 months). Identity lock-in (rituals, name).
↓
SPACES VALUE DELIVERY (Spinks)
↓ Audit SPACES outcomes. Which 2-3 underserved? Invest.
↓
LEADER IDENTIFICATION (Richardson)
↓ Who keeps showing up? Mark for leadership.
↓
PASS PHASE (Richardson 3 steps)
↓ (6) Distribute leadership WITH not FOR. (7) Supercharge. (8) Celebrate.
↓ Growth: 200→500 members (6 months). 5 sub-leaders. Distributed.
↓
FLYWHEEL MOMENTUM (Collins)
↓ Each turn: Consistent push → member value → new members attracted
↓ Turn 4 = self-sustaining. No founder push needed.
↓
GROWTH ENGINE AMPLIFICATION (Ferriss/Weinberg)
↓ Channel community as traction. WOM + referrals + content.
↓
EXPONENTIAL SCALING (12 months)
↓ Turn 1: 50. Turn 2: 100. Turn 3: 300. Turn 4: 500. Turn ∞: 1000+
```

## Agent Prompts

### Prompt 1: COMMUNITY VISION

```
"Define community vision:
- WHO (target member): Specific persona (not everyone). What unites them?
- WHY (shared purpose): What do they care about? Problem they're solving?
- IDENTITY: Name? Logo? Values? Monthly rituals?
- CATEGORY (Positioning): 'X community for Y people who Z.'
Output: Community charter (1-page vision + identity)."
```

### Prompt 2: SPARK LAUNCH

```
"Launch community Spark phase:
1. Recruit 50 founding members (most passionate/connected).
2. DO something together (weekly ritual): Zoom call, workshop, challenge.
3. Horizontal conversation: Peer-to-peer (not founder lecturing).
4. Set expectations: What will we do together? What value will you get?
Output: Launch playbook (recruitment + first 4 weeks)."
```

### Prompt 3: APPRECIATION SYSTEM

```
"Design appreciation ritual:
- Public recognition: 'Shout out [name] for [specific achievement]. Insane.'
- Socratic questions: 'How did you solve X?' (let them teach others).
- Impact show: 'Your advice helped 3 members close deals.'
- Listen more: 4 ears vs 1 mouth. Ask questions, don't lecture.
Output: Recognition templates + weekly shout-out schedule."
```

### Prompt 4: SPACES AUDIT

```
"Audit SPACES outcomes (Spinks):
- Support: Members helping members? Q&A threads active? Mentoring?
- Product: Community influencing product roadmap? Feedback loops?
- Acquisition: WOM referrals? 'Tell a friend' working? Net new members?
- Contribution: UGC (member case studies, guides, content)? Co-creation?
- Engagement: 70%+ monthly active? Sticky? Return rate high?
- Success: Members growing (business, skills, network)? Outcomes tracked?
For each: Rate 1-10. Which 2-3 underserved? Action plan to improve.
Output: SPACES scorecard + investment priorities."
```

### Prompt 5: LEADER IDENTIFICATION + SUPERCHARGE

```
"Identify + supercharge leaders:
IDENTIFICATION: Who in community keeps showing up?
- High engagement (posts, helps others, attends calls)
- Status (others respect, follow)
- Type: Helper (answers Q&A), Organizer (runs events), Speaker (shares insights)
SUPERCHARGE: Give autonomy + visibility + tools.
- Ravi (helper): 'Lead Q&A thread Fridays?'
- Priya (organizer): 'Organize monthly meetup?'
- Anmol (speaker): 'Record case study video?'
- Each: Public credit ('Thanks to our leaders...').
Output: Leader roster + delegated ownership."
```

### Prompt 6: GROWTH CHANNEL STRATEGY

```
"Amplify community via Growth Engine (Ferriss DEAL + Weinberg Bullseye):
1. DEFINITION: Growth target (1000 members by month 12?). Timeline.
2. ELIMINATION: What 80% of tactics NOT to do? Focus time.
3. CHANNEL BULLSEYE: Test community as traction channel (vs PR, content, SEM).
   - Outer: Brainstorm 19 channels
   - Middle: Rank by effort/reach/confidence
   - Inner: Top 3 (Content marketing + Word of mouth + Email)
4. AUTOMATION: Refer-a-friend program (members bring members). Email sequence.
5. LIBERATION: Community self-grows (referral loop, word-of-mouth). No founder push.
Output: Growth strategy for community (member acquisition, retention)."
```

## 12-Month Roadmap

### Months 1-3: SPARK + BUILD IDENTITY

- Weeks 1-4: Recruit 50 founding members. Weekly Zoom ritual.
- Weeks 5-8: Horizontal conversation (peer-to-peer). Identity cement (name, values, rituals).
- Weeks 9-12: Hit 100 members. 1 sub-leader emerging (organizer).
- Metrics: Retention 70%+, engagement >50% weekly active.

### Months 4-6: STOKE + DISTRIBUTE LEADERSHIP

- Identify 3-5 leaders (helpers, organizers, speakers).
- Give autonomy: Q&A threads, monthly meetup, case study videos.
- Audit SPACES: Hit 2 underserved outcomes (Contribution + Acquisition).
- Growth: 100→300 members. Referral program launched.
- Metrics: Retention 75%+, NPS 50+, 40% new members from referrals.

### Months 7-9: PASS + FLYWHEEL MOMENTUM

- Leadership distributed. Founder facilitates, members drive.
- Content from members (case studies, guides). UGC strong.
- Community as growth channel. Amplify via email + partner channels.
- Growth: 300→500 members. 5 sub-leaders. Self-sustaining indicators emerging.
- Metrics: Retention 80%+, 50% new members from referrals, community driving 30% new customers.

### Months 10-12: EXPONENTIAL + SUSTAINABILITY

- Flywheel hitting exponential turn (each turn = 2x output).
- No founder push needed. Rituals self-running. Leadership deep.
- Community → largest traction channel. Word-of-mouth amplifying.
- Growth: 500→1000 members. Breakeven (self-sustaining). Roadmap 2-year plan.
- Metrics: Retention 85%+, 60% new members from referrals, community = revenue driver.

## Example Build

**Community**: Indie SaaS Builders (solo founders shipping products).

### Vision

- WHO: Solo founders (1-person SaaS companies). Building in public.
- WHY: Accountability + feedback + co-founder search + peer support.
- IDENTITY: "Indie SaaS Builders" (name). Values: Shipping, learning, helping. Ritual: Weekly "Ship Day" Zoom (30 min, share what you built).

### Spark Launch

- Recruit: 50 most connected solo founders (Twitter, ProductHunt, Twitter).
- DO: Weekly "Ship Day" zoom (show progress, give feedback, celebrate).
- Tone: Peer-to-peer (no lectures, no selling).

### Appreciation

- Shout: "@Sam shipped [product name]. Revenue-positive in 2 weeks. Insane."
- Ask: "How did you find your first co-founder?" (let him teach).
- Show: "Your advice helped 3 members hit $1K MRR."

### SPACES Audit

- Support: 8/10 (great peer help in Q&A)
- Product: 4/10 (no feedback loop to product roadmap yet)
- Acquisition: 5/10 (some referrals, not systematic)
- Contribution: 6/10 (few case studies, need more UGC)
- Engagement: 8/10 (70% weekly active in Ship Day calls)
- Success: 9/10 (members average $5K MRR growth)

### Leader Identification + Supercharge

- Ravi (helper): Leads Q&A thread (8/week). → "Lead Q&A Friday community call?"
- Priya (organizer): Organizes co-founder matching. → "Organize monthly in-person meetup?"
- Anmol (speaker): Shares insights. → "Record monthly case study video?"

### Growth Strategy

- Target: 1000 members month 12.
- Channels: (1) Content marketing (blog + Twitter threads), (2) Word-of-mouth (referral program), (3) Email (cold outreach to similar founders).
- Automation: Refer-a-friend (members refer → 1-month free).
- Result: Month 12 = 50 new members/month from referrals (passive, founder-independent).
