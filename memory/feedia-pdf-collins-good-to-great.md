# Jim Collins — Good to Great (2001)

**Core**: Good = complacency trap. Rare companies leap to great (6.9x market return, 15+ years). Systematic patterns identified via research.

## 9 Key Findings

1. **Level 5 Leadership** — Paradox: humble + fierce determination. Credit others, take blame.
2. **First Who Then What** — Get right people on bus before strategy. People > planning.
3. **Confront Brutal Facts** — Faith in victory (long-term) + harsh reality (short-term). No denial.
4. **Hedgehog Concept** — 3 circles (understand deeply, passionate, drives economy). One focused idea.
5. **Culture of Discipline** — Self-disciplined people. Say no to good ideas. Freedom within framework.
6. **Technology Accelerators** — Amplifies what exists (aligned with Hedgehog), not cause.
7. **Flywheel** — Consistent push → momentum builds exponentially. No single breakthrough.
8. **Sustainability** — 15+ years via discipline, culture, succession planning.
9. **Built to Last** — From great to enduring greatness.

## 11 Good-to-Great Companies

Walgreens, Kimberly-Clark, Nucor, Southwest, Abbott Labs, Fannie Mae, Gillette, Bristol Myers, Wells Fargo, Philip Morris, Kroger.

Results: 6.9x market return over 15-year sustaining period (vs comparison companies 1.3x).
