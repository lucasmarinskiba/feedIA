# David Spinks — The Business of Belonging (2021)

**Core**: Community = members ACT together. Audience ≠ community. Business value = SPACES outcomes + distributed control.

**4 Sense of Community Factors** (McMillan & Chavis):

1. Membership — boundaries, emotional safety, belonging, personal investment, symbols
2. Influence — bidirectional power (members influence community, community influences members)
3. Integration + Needs Fulfillment — solves why members joined
4. Shared Emotional Connection — history, experiences, crisis creates bonds

**SPACES Model** (6 Business Outcomes):

- Support (customer support, members answering members)
- Product (innovation, feedback, R&D)
- Acquisition (marketing, word-of-mouth, SEO)
- Contribution (user-generated content, platforms)
- Engagement (retention, loyalty, switching costs)
- Success (education, skill building, career growth)

**3 Etapas + 9 Steps**:

1. Spark: Pinpoint people + Do together + Get talking
2. Stoke: Attract aligned + Cultivate identity + Observe leaders
3. Pass: Create more leaders + Supercharge + Celebrate

**Key Principles**:

- Build WITH not FOR (autonomy vs top-down)
- Distribution of control (push to edges, maintain guiderails)
- Social capital = relaciones reciprocidad (asset like physical/human capital)
- Sustainability = business model required (funding = condition existence)
- Bonding vs Bridging (similarity vs diversity)

**Anti-patterns**: No shared action first, generic identity, CM as only node, vanity metrics, underinvestment.
