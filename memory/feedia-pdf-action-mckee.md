# Robert McKee & Bassim El-Wakil — Action (2022)

**Core**: Action = altruistic hero vs narcissistic villain over Life/Death. Excitement (not terror) comes from hero's selflessness even in lethal jeopardy.

**Core Cast**:

- **Hero** = Altruism (sacrifices own life for innocent)
- **Villain** = Narcissism (indifference to humanity, obsessed with own vision)
- **Victim** = Helplessness (can't save self, physical/circumstantial limit)

**Core Value**: Life/Death binary drives tension.
**Core Emotion**: EXCITEMENT (safe-distance danger, hero's altruism keeps audience emotionally distanced from hero peril).

**10 Principal Genres + Action Intersections**:
Crime (Justice/Injustice), War (Victory/Defeat), Epic (Tyranny/Freedom), Horror (Survival/Damnation), Political (Power/Weakness), Love (Lasting/Lost), Enterprise (Success/Failure), Social (Problem/Solution), Domestic (Unity/Breakup).

**Mercy Scene** (Core Event):

- Hero HELPLESS under villain power
- Villain delays kill (narcissism = gloating, torture, plan revelation)
- Hero outsmarts OR overpowers (no coincidence)

**Action Subgenres** (4 Types):

1. Adventure (natural forces, exotic, quest)
2. Epic (vs tyranny/empire)
3. Duel (hero vs villain one-on-one)
4. Thriller (vs psychopathic villain, visceral)

**Excitement Sources** (5):

1. Rebellion vs Authority
2. Exploring Unknown
3. Coping with Frustration
4. Conquering Limitations
5. Breaking Taboos

**Anti-patterns**: Absent villain (henchmen mercy scenes), obvious Achilles heel, clichéd distractions, coincidence saves, deus ex machina.
