# Robert Cialdini — Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion (1984/1993)

**Core**: Humanos tienen fixed-action patterns triggered por cues específicas (como turkeys respond to "tchip-tchip"). Triggers override reasoning.

**Guiding Principle**: "Click-whirr" = automatic response (como cassette activando). Humans = automatic AND rational, depending contexto.

## 6 Weapons of Influence

1. **Reciprocity** — Giving = obligation return. Give first → creates "debt". Disproportionate: small gift → large return.
2. **Commitment & Consistency** — Small asks → escalating. "Foot in door" escalates. Written > verbal.
3. **Social Proof** — "People like us do things like this." Testimonials from SIMILAR people > expert. Uncertainty = look a otros.
4. **Authority** — Titles, credentials, uniforms trigger compliance. Specific credentials (Dr. 10 years) > general. Third-party > self-proclamation.
5. **Liking** — Compliance con gente que nos gusta. Similarity, compliments, shared goals = bond.
6. **Scarcity** — Rare = valuable (real o artificial). "Only 3 left" > "plenty". Exclusivity > mass. Loss > gain frame.

## Fixed-Action Pattern Examples

**Turkey Experiment** (M.W. Fox):

- Turkey attacks weasel (natural enemy).
- Turkey mothers baby turkeys when they emit "tchip-tchip" sound.
- Weasel puppet WITHOUT sound → turkey attacks.
- Weasel puppet WITH "tchip-tchip" recording → turkey adopts weasel.
- Stop recording → turkey attacks again.

**The trigger**: Sound (not actual baby turkey). Automatic bypass reason.

**Human Parallel** (Ellen Langer):

- "Can I cut line?" → 60% yes.
- "Can I cut line because I'm in rush?" → 94% yes.
- "Can I cut line because I need make copies?" (no real reason) → 93% yes.

**The trigger**: Word "because" itself activates automatic compliance (even no real reason).
