# Robert McKee — Character (2021)

**Core**: Character ≠ Person. People experience more than express. Characters express everything they experience (MORE complex than real humans).

**Character vs Person**:

- Person = work-in-progress, 24/7 life
- Character = finished art, exists between curtain up/down, fade in/out
- Character = MORE layered, complex, memorable (time to study them)

**Characterization vs True Character**:

- **Characterization** = outer traits (age, gender, dress, speech, personality, job) — MASK
- **True Character** = inner nature (morality, values, deepest drives) — REVEALED through risk-filled choices under pressure

**Principle of Choice**: Character expresses true self through RISK-FILLED decisions under maximum stakes. No stakes = choice meaningless. Maximum stakes = choice most revealing.

**10 Faculties of Writers**:

1. Taste (disgust with banality, eye for vital)
2. Knowledge (observe, research, read, travel, experience)
3. Originality (unearth hidden truths, combine influences)
4. Showmanship (entertain + reveal)
5. Awareness of reader/audience (moment-by-moment impact)
6. Mastery of Form (story form first)
7. Hatred of Clichés (know history, innovate)
8. Moral Imagination (understand binaries deeply)
9. Ideal Self (best possible self when writing)
10. Self-Knowledge (all character creation begins/ends in own humanity)

**Dimensions = Contradiction**:

- Flat = one consistent trait
- Dimensional = opposing traits creating contradiction
- Complex = 2+ dimensions revealing layers (Walter White: teacher vs kingpin)

**Character Creation Methods**:

- **Outside-In** (Invention) — behavior/trait → psychological root
- **Inside-Out** (Birth) — character emerges mysteriously in scenes

**4 Researches**:

1. Personal (own emotional experiences transformed)
2. Imaginative (observe 360°, don't list traits)
3. Book-Bound (psychology, sociology, history validation)
4. Grounded (locate in reality, witness, become authority)

**Archetypes**:
Protagonist, Antagonist, Mentor, Ally, Trickster, Guardian.

**Anti-patterns**: Cardboard roles, no dimensions, psychological clichés, melodrama, author mouthpiece, no research, vague characterization.
