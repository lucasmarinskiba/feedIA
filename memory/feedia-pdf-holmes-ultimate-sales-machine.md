# Chet Holmes — Ultimate Sales Machine (2007)

**Core**: Mastery ≠ gifted. Mastery = pigheaded discipline + repetición hasta automático.

**Guiding Principle**: "Doing handful of moves 4,000 times > 4,000 moves once."

## 12 Key Strategies

1. **Time Management** — Billionaire secrets. Energy peaks. Calendar blocking.
2. **Higher Standards & Training** — Preprogram responses. Every scenario = script.
3. **Effective Meetings** — Workshop training. Structured collaboration.
4. **Brilliant Strategy** — 9x impact via focus. Leverage points over brute force.
5. **Hiring Superstars** — High-octane talent at every level. Multiplier.
6. **Best Buyers** — Fast + cheap customer acquisition.
7. **7 Musts of Marketing** — Turbocharge all marketing efforts.
8. **Eyes Have It** — Compelling visuals. Presentation mistakes.
9. **Day-by-Day Tactics** — Specifics to land dream clients.
10. **Sales Skills** — Deeper = more sales. Objection handling. Follow-up scripts.
11. **Follow-up & Bonding** — Keep clients forever. Loyalty = profit multiplier.
12. **Systems & Goals** — Measurement, activation, execution.

## Karate Analogy

Holmes entrenaba con bouncing ball en techo. Mes 1 = hits head constantly. Mes 3 = blocks most. Mes 6 = never touches.

**Why?** Not 4,000 different moves. Same defensive moves, 4,000 times. Body = machine.

**Apply to business**: Sales team no necesita 4,000 objection responses. Necesita 12 core responses, executed 4,000 times.

12 STRATEGIES: Focus relentlessly. Not 4,000 tactics.
SYSTEMS: Every situation has script. No winging it.
REPETITION: Same moves 4,000 times = muscle memory.
TEAM: Everyone trained on core responses. Consistency.
FOLLOW-UP: System for every contact. Keep forever.
OBJECTION HANDLING: 10 responses per objection (practiced, automatic).
KNOWING ≠ DOING: Gap = where failure lives.
